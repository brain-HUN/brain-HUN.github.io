gdoc-edit

gdoc-edit is a personal command-line tool for reading and editing my own Google Docs through the Google Docs and Google Drive APIs. It runs only on my own computer and in my own development sessions, and is used only by its author (akos.brain@gmail.com). It is not offered to other users.

What it does with Google data: it downloads a document I choose, lets me edit it locally, and sends back only the changes I make. It can also download, upload or replace images in that document.
